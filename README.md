# Nothing Got Fixed

An analysis of whether Indian on-demand delivery apps act on the complaints
users leave in public Google Play reviews.

**Live:** https://rvshankar45-jpg.github.io/ravishankar.github.io/

325,715 public Play Store reviews for Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit and Zepto were
collected over 1 July – 21 September 2026. The 43,649 complaints among them
were sorted into four categories — price, product, delivery and app — and
each was tracked release by release.

Headline: no category, in any app, showed a sustained decline. Three of the
four apps reply to ~98% of their one- and two-star reviews within minutes,
and how much a company replies has no detectable relationship with whether
its complaints go down.

Classification by Jev AI (TypeSafe AI) on the hardest cases, distilled into a
local classifier for the rest — 91.4% agreement on held-out reviews.

No reviewer names were stored, and only aggregate figures are published.
