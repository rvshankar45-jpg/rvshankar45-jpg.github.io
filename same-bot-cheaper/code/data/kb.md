# Leafy Customer Support Knowledge Base

Leafy is an online plant store selling live houseplants, pots, soil and accessories. This document is the source of truth for support. If something is not covered here, do not guess: offer to escalate (Section 8).

## 1. Orders

- Orders are placed on the website or app only. Order numbers look like `LF-` plus six digits (e.g. LF-204518).
- Orders can be changed or cancelled free within **2 hours** of ordering, from "My Orders". After that the order is in the packing queue and can't be changed online; support can try to intercept it before it ships, but this is not guaranteed.
- Shipped orders cannot be cancelled, and the shipping address cannot be changed after shipping. Couriers do not accept redirection requests from Leafy.
- **Out of stock after ordering:** we email within 24 hours offering a similar plant of equal or higher value, a wait of up to 14 days, or a refund for that item. No reply within 3 days means an automatic refund.
- Customers must be 18 or older to hold an account.
- Gift orders get a free printed gift note (up to 200 characters). Prices are never printed on gift packing slips.
- Plants ship in plastic nursery pots with drainage holes. Decorative pots are sold separately unless the listing says "potted".

## 2. Returns

- **Non-living items** (pots, tools, soil, fertiliser, grow lights, accessories) can be returned within **30 days** of delivery, unused and in original packaging. Opened soil or fertiliser can't be returned unless faulty.
- **Live plants cannot be returned** for change of mind. Plant problems go through the Plant Care Guarantee (Section 4) or Damaged on Arrival (Section 5).
- Start a return from "My Orders". A prepaid label is emailed; labels expire after **7 days**, so drop the parcel off within 7 days.
- Faulty or incorrect items return free. Change-of-mind returns cost a flat **$6.95**, deducted from the refund (free for Leafy Plus members).
- Refunds are issued within **5 business days** of the return reaching our warehouse, to the original payment method. Original shipping is refunded only if the whole order is returned because of our error.
- No direct exchanges: return the item and place a new order.

## 3. Shipping

| Zone | Distance from nearest greenhouse | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Under 300 miles | 2–3 business days | Next business day |
| 2 | 300–1,000 miles | 3–5 business days | 2 business days |
| 3 | Over 1,000 miles, remote and island addresses | 5–8 business days | Not available |

- **Standard:** $7.95, free on orders of **$75+** (after discounts, before tax). **Express:** $14.95, never free, including for Leafy Plus.
- **XL plants** (usually over 4 ft) add a **$20** handling fee and ship Standard only.
- **Live plants ship Monday–Wednesday only.** Plant orders placed after 12 noon Wednesday ship the next Monday. Non-living items ship Monday–Friday; mixed orders ship together on a plant day.
- **Weather holds:** if transit forecasts are below 35°F or above 95°F, live plants may be held up to 7 days and the customer is emailed. Customers can waive the hold by replying; the Plant Care Guarantee then still applies except for cold or heat damage in transit, and DOA claims for cold or heat damage are not accepted. A free heat pack can be added at checkout in winter.
- **Tracking** is emailed on shipping and can take up to 24 hours to update. If it hasn't moved for **4 business days**, or shows delivered but nothing arrived, contact support: we open a courier investigation (up to **5 business days**). If lost, the customer chooses a free replacement or a full refund.
- No international shipping. PO boxes are for non-living items only; live plants can't go to PO boxes.

## 4. Plant Care Guarantee

- Every live plant has a **30-day guarantee** from delivery (**90 days** for Leafy Plus members, on plants ordered while the membership is active).
- If the plant dies or declines significantly despite reasonable care, we send **one** free replacement of the same plant (store credit if out of stock). Significant decline means losing more than a third of its leaves, stem or root rot, or collapse. Yellowing of one or two older leaves in the first two weeks is normal transplant stress and not covered on its own.
- **Claim** from "My Orders" within the window with at least **two photos** (whole plant + close-up). A Plant Care Specialist reviews within **2 business days**. The plant does not need to be sent back.
- **Not covered:** repotting into non-Leafy soil in the first 14 days; pets, pests introduced after delivery or deliberate neglect; cold or heat damage in transit when the weather hold was waived; claims after the window.
- A replacement plant gets its own new 30-day guarantee, so it can be replaced once more if it fails; the plant sent as that second replacement has no guarantee.
- Anyone, customer or not, can get free care advice from a Plant Care Specialist by chat or email.

## 5. Damaged or Wrong Items on Arrival (DOA)

- DOA means snapped main stems, a cracked or smashed pot, roots exposed, or a dead or severely wilted plant; or a broken non-living item. A few bent leaves or loose soil is normal and not damage.
- Report within **48 hours** of delivery with photos of the item and the box. After 48 hours, plant issues go through the Plant Care Guarantee.
- DOA plant: free replacement on the next plant shipping day, or a refund. DOA non-living item: replacement or refund; items under **$40** usually don't need to be sent back. DOA replacements don't count as the guarantee's one replacement.
- **Wrong item:** we ship the correct item free by Express (where the zone allows) with a free return label. A wrong live plant can be kept at no charge.

## 6. Payments and Refunds

- Accepted: credit and debit cards, digital wallets at checkout, Leafy gift cards. No cash, cheques or bank transfers. Buy-now-pay-later is available on orders of **$50–$1,000**.
- Cards are authorised at checkout and charged when the order ships; an order shipped in several parcels can show one charge per parcel. A pending authorisation on a cancelled order drops off in **3–7 business days**, depending on the bank; Leafy can't release it faster.
- **Duplicate charges:** first check whether one is a pending authorisation. If both have posted, the customer sends the order number and last four card digits, and the duplicate is refunded within **3 business days** of confirmation. Never ask for a full card number, security code or password.
- **Refunds go only to the original payment method**, never a different card, even if the original is closed or expired; the bank normally passes it to the replacement card. Alternatively the customer can take **Leafy store credit**, issued instantly and never expiring. Buy-now-pay-later refunds go to the instalment provider, which adjusts remaining payments.
- Refund timing once issued: cards **5–10 business days**, digital wallets **3–5 business days**, store credit instant.
- **Price drop within 7 days** of purchase: the difference as store credit, once per item, excluding flash sales and clearance.
- **Promo codes:** one per order; can't be added after ordering; can't be combined with Leafy Plus member pricing on the same item (the better discount applies automatically).
- **Gift cards:** $25–$500, never expire, not refundable for cash. Lost codes are reissued if the purchaser gives the gift card order number.

## 7. Subscriptions and Membership

- **Plant Club:** one surprise plant a month for the customer's light level. **Starter** $29/month (small plant, 4-inch pot); **Collector** $49/month (medium or rare plant, 6-inch pot). Charged on the **25th** of the previous month; boxes ship in the first full week of the month. Customers can't choose the plant but can list up to 3 plants they own to avoid duplicates. Plant Club plants have the normal guarantee.
- Skip, pause (up to 3 months), change plan or cancel from "Account" > "Subscriptions". Changes before the 25th affect the next box; changes on or after the 25th apply to the following month's box. No cancellation fee or minimum term. An already-billed box is not refunded on cancellation and is still sent.
- **Leafy Plus:** **$59/year**, auto-renews. Free Standard shipping with no minimum, free change-of-mind returns, 90-day guarantee, 10% member pricing on pots and accessories, early access to rare plant drops. No free Express. Full refund if cancelled within **14 days** of purchase or renewal **and** no benefits used; otherwise no refund, and benefits continue to the end of the paid year.

## 8. Contact and Escalation

- **Chat:** 8am–8pm Mon–Sat. **Email:** help@leafy.example, answered within 1 business day. **Plant Care Specialists:** chat 9am–5pm Mon–Fri. No phone support. Times are the customer's local time.
- **Escalate to a human Support Lead** when: there is a charge dispute or suspected fraud; the customer wants something outside policy and is upset or insists; the same issue has been raised three or more times; a refund or credit over **$150** is involved; or there is a safety issue.
- **Safety:** if a person or pet may have eaten part of a plant, first tell them to contact their doctor, vet or local poison control immediately, then escalate.
- **Goodwill:** agents may offer a one-time store credit of up to **$15** for a poor experience (e.g. a late delivery or a delayed reply); more needs a Support Lead. Goodwill is never cash.
- Be polite, empathetic and honest, include timeframes, and never promise what policy doesn't allow; if an exception might be possible, say you will escalate.
- **Account:** data can be downloaded or deleted under "Account" > "Privacy"; deletion takes up to 30 days and cancels subscriptions. Support can't change an account email over chat; customers change it themselves after logging in. If an account may have been accessed by someone else, the customer should reset their password immediately and support escalates.

## 9. Plant Safety and Care

- Not pet-safe: monstera, pothos, peace lily, snake plant, ZZ plant, fiddle leaf fig. Pet-safe: calathea, spider plant, parlour palm, Boston fern, peperomia.
- Every plant ships with a care card. Keep new plants out of direct sun for the first week and don't repot for 2–3 weeks. Most early problems come from overwatering: water only when the top inch of soil is dry.
